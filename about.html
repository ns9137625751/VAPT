Why we exist

Most penetration test reports are scanner output with a logo on the front. They arrive weeks late, list two hundred findings nobody has verified, and get filed unread because the engineering team cannot tell which three items actually matter.

We started in 2019 after enough years on the other side of that transaction. The premise is narrow and we have not changed it: a security assessment is only worth what your engineers can act on. So every finding is exploited before it is written down, every finding ships with a fix, and the retest is included rather than sold back to you six months later.

We are a small team by choice. Every engagement is run by the people who will write the report, and you talk to them directly for its whole duration.

[x]

If we think a VAPT is the wrong spend for your situation, we say so on the scoping call. We have talked several teams out of an engagement and into fixing their build pipeline instead.

How we work

[+] Proof or it is not real Nothing enters the report as a vulnerability without a working proof-of-concept and retained evidence.
[+] Two testers, always A second tester peer-reviews the finding set before delivery. It is how false positives and inflated severities die.
[+] Fixed scope, fixed price The quote you sign is the invoice you receive. Scope changes require a signed amendment, not a surprise line item.
[+] Findings arrive live Criticals are reported the hour they are confirmed. Waiting for the report to tell you the building is on fire is negligent.
[+] Retest included One full retest within 30 days of delivery, priced into the engagement rather than sold as an upsell.
[+] Your data stays yours Evidence encrypted at rest, never used for marketing, and destroyed on request with written confirmation.
[-] No resale, no referral fees We do not sell tooling and we take no commission for recommending one. The advice is not compromised.

The team

Nine testers, two report editors, one operations lead. Everyone who tests holds at least one hands-on practical certification — multiple-choice qualifications do not count here.

OSCP / OSWE / OSEP Offensive Security practicals — held by every tester on the web and network side.
CREST CRT / CCT Registered and Certified Tester, for engagements where the client or regulator requires CREST-accredited personnel.
GWAPT / GPEN / GMOB GIAC web, network and mobile specialisations across the respective service lines.
CCSP / cloud practitioner Provider-specific security certification for AWS, Azure and GCP assessments.
Background checks Every tester is DBS or equivalent checked and under a standing NDA before joining an engagement.
