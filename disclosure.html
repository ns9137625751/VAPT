Responsible disclosure policy.

We run a security practice, so it would be embarrassing to make it hard to report a problem in our own systems. Report in good faith and we will treat your research as authorised.

We run a security practice, so it would be embarrassing to make it hard to report a problem in our own systems. If you have found something, we want to hear about it and we will not come after you for telling us.

We aim to acknowledge every report within one business day, give an initial assessment within five, and keep you updated until it is closed.

What is in scope

[+] vapt-security.com This site and every subdomain we operate.
[+] Client portal portal.vapt-security.com — report delivery and evidence access.
[+] Public API api.vapt-security.com — findings export endpoints.
[+] Email security SPF, DKIM, DMARC and spoofing issues affecting our domains.

What is out of scope

These are either not ours to fix, not vulnerabilities, or would harm people who did not consent. Reports limited to the following will be closed without an award.

  • Denial of service, volumetric testing or resource exhaustion
  • Social engineering or phishing of our staff, clients or suppliers
  • Physical attacks against our offices or personnel
  • Systems belonging to our clients — report those to the client
  • Third-party services we merely consume, unless our configuration is at fault
  • Missing security headers with no demonstrated impact
  • Scanner output submitted without a working proof-of-concept
  • Best-practice opinions, such as TLS cipher preferences with no exploit

Safe harbour

If you make a good-faith effort to comply with this policy during your research, we will consider it authorised, we will not pursue or support legal action against you, and if a third party brings action we will make clear that you were acting within our policy.

Good faith means all of the following:

  • You stay within the in-scope systems listed above
  • You stop at proof of concept and do not pivot further
  • You access only the minimum data needed to demonstrate the issue
  • You do not modify, exfiltrate, retain or destroy any data
  • You do not degrade service for anyone else
  • You report promptly and give us reasonable time to fix before disclosing
[!]

If you access personal data by accident, stop, tell us immediately, and delete your copy. Say so in the report — it will not count against you.

How to report

Email security@vapt-security.com, encrypted with the key below if the finding is sensitive. A useful report contains:

  • The affected URL, endpoint or host
  • Reproduction steps precise enough for us to follow exactly
  • What an attacker could do with it, in one paragraph
  • Any request captures, screenshots or scripts you used
  • How you would like to be credited, or that you would rather not be
Acknowledgement Within 1 business day.
Initial assessment Within 5 business days, including our severity view.
Fix target Critical 7 days · high 30 days · medium 90 days.
Public disclosure Coordinated with you, normally 90 days after the report or on fix, whichever comes first.
Reward We do not run a paid bounty. We offer credit, a written reference, and our genuine thanks.
