The basics

What is VAPT?

VAPT stands for Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing. It is a security check that finds weaknesses in your technology before attackers do. It is two jobs, not one.

Vulnerability Assessment — find the weaknesses

We scan your systems and applications to build a list of known security weaknesses and possible risks. This tells you what could go wrong.

Penetration Testing — safely simulate a real attack

Our security experts try to use those weaknesses the way a real attacker would, with your written permission. This tells you what would actually happen.

    How it works

    Our VAPT process

    Seven steps, agreed in writing before we start. You always know what is being tested, when, and by whom.

    1

    Understand

    We learn what your systems do, who uses them, and what matters most to your business.

    2

    Plan

    We agree the scope, rules, timing and contact people, in writing, before anything starts.

    3

    Assess

    Our team runs automated scans and then tests by hand for the problems tools cannot find.

    4

    Validate

    We prove each issue is real and remove false alarms, so you do not waste time on noise.

    5

    Report

    You get a clear report: what we found, why it matters, the evidence, and how to fix it.

    6

    Fix

    We support your developers while they fix things, and answer questions as they go.

    7

    Retest

    We test everything again to confirm the fixes worked, and issue an updated report.
