Retest

Once you have shipped fixes we re-run the full finding set and issue an updated report showing what closed. Included in the engagement price, not billed separately.

[08] of [08]   +30 days

What this phase produces

Nothing moves to sign-off until every line below is done and recorded.

  • Every finding re-tested, not just the ones you flagged as fixed
  • Regression check against the original baseline
  • Updated report and attestation letter issued
  • Findings that remain open are re-rated with current context

Questions about how we run retest?

The rules of engagement are agreed in writing before day one, and they can be tightened to whatever your risk team needs. Ask us on the scoping call.
