What we collect

Enquiry details Name, work email, company, role and whatever you write in the message field, when you submit a form on this site.
Newsletter address Your email address, if you subscribe to the advisories digest. Nothing else.
Server logs IP address, user agent, requested path and timestamp, retained for 30 days for security and abuse handling.
Engagement data Whatever your scope requires us to touch. Covered separately below, because it is the sensitive part.
[-] Not collected No advertising identifiers, no cross-site tracking, no data brokers, no profiles built about you.

Engagement data

Penetration testing means we will encounter data belonging to your organisation, and sometimes to your customers. This is the part of the policy that actually matters.

  • We access the minimum needed to demonstrate a finding, and no further
  • Evidence is redacted at capture wherever the finding can still be proven
  • All evidence is encrypted at rest and in transit
  • Only testers assigned to your engagement can access it
  • We never use your data to train models or to market to anyone
  • We never disclose that you are a client without written permission
[x]

For engagements involving personal data we act as your processor, under a DPA that sits alongside the statement of work. See the DPA summary.

Lawful basis

Contract Engagement data and client contact details — processed to deliver the assessment you commissioned.
Legitimate interests Enquiry handling and server logs — to respond to you and to keep our own systems secure.
Consent The advisories newsletter. Withdraw it with the unsubscribe link in any issue, or by emailing us.
Legal obligation Invoices and financial records, retained for the period tax law requires.

Retention and deletion

Raw evidence Destroyed 30 days after the retest closes, or immediately on written request. We confirm destruction in writing.
Final reports Retained 12 months so we can support your auditors, then destroyed unless you ask us to keep them.
Enquiries 24 months from last contact, then deleted.
Newsletter Until you unsubscribe, then removed within 30 days.
Server logs 30 days.
Invoices 7 years, as required by tax law.

Who we share with

We do not sell data, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing. The complete list of processors is:

  • Our email provider, for correspondence and the newsletter
  • Our hosting provider, for this site and the client portal
  • Our accountant, for invoices only
  • Law enforcement, only where we are legally compelled and, where permitted, only after telling you

All processors are contractually bound to equivalent protections. A current list with locations is available on request.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, EU GDPR and comparable regimes you can ask us to:

  • Confirm what we hold about you and give you a copy
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Delete it, where no legal obligation requires us to keep it
  • Restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Export it in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for anything based on consent

Email privacy@vapt-security.com. We respond within 30 days and do not charge. If we get it wrong you can complain to your supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. There is no analytics script, no tag manager, no advertising pixel and no consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to.

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. If you would rather it did not, the fonts can be self-hosted — the page falls back to your system monospace and is fully readable either way.

The client portal sets one strictly necessary session cookie. It carries no tracking identifier and expires when you log out.
