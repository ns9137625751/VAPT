Engaging us

Each engagement is governed by a signed statement of work that names the scope, dates, price and contacts. These terms apply alongside it. Where they conflict, the statement of work wins.

  • Quotes are valid for 30 days from issue
  • Work begins when the statement of work is signed and dates are booked
  • Scope changes require a written amendment signed by both sides
  • Either party may sign an NDA before any scoping detail is exchanged

Authorisation and scope

Penetration testing is only lawful with authorisation from the party that owns the systems. By signing a statement of work you confirm that you own the in-scope assets, or hold written permission from whoever does.

  • We test only what the scope sheet names, and nothing outside it
  • If your systems sit on third-party infrastructure, you obtain that provider's authorisation before testing begins
  • Testing occurs only inside the agreed window and from agreed addresses
  • Either side may invoke the emergency stop, halting testing within 15 minutes
[!]

If we find we are testing an asset you do not in fact control, we stop immediately and tell you. We do not continue on assurances given verbally.

Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate scope information and working test credentials for every role
  • Take a verified backup before testing begins
  • Nominate an escalation contact reachable during the testing window
  • Tell us about fragile components so we can adjust rather than break them
  • Notify any third parties whose authorisation the engagement requires

Delays caused by missing credentials or environments are the most common reason an engagement slips. Where a booked window is lost to them, we may need to rebook.

What testing cannot promise

An honest statement of the limits, because a vendor who claims otherwise is selling you something that does not exist.

  • A test is a point-in-time assessment of a defined scope, not a guarantee that no vulnerabilities exist
  • Coverage is bounded by the time, access and scope agreed
  • Passing a test is not a warranty against future compromise, and code changed after delivery is untested
  • Compliance mapping supports your audit; it is not itself a certification
  • Findings reflect the environment as configured at the time of testing

Deliverables and use

Ownership The report is yours on payment. Our methodology, tooling and templates remain ours.
Internal use Share the report freely inside your organisation and with your auditors, insurers and regulators.
External use Share with customers and prospects under NDA. The attestation letter exists for wider distribution.
Attribution Do not publish extracts in a way that implies we endorse a product or a security posture we did not assess.
Our references We name you as a client only with written permission. Advisories are always anonymised.

Fees and cancellation

Pricing Fixed scope, fixed price. The quote you sign is the invoice you receive.
Payment Net 30 from delivery, unless the statement of work says otherwise.
Retest One full retest within 30 days of delivery, included in the price. Further retests are quoted separately.
Cancellation Free more than 10 business days before the booked window; 50% within 10 days; 100% once testing has begun.
Rebooking One free reschedule with more than 5 business days' notice.

Liability

We carry professional indemnity and cyber liability insurance; certificates are available on request. Our aggregate liability for any engagement is limited to the fees paid for that engagement, except where the law does not permit such a limit — including death or personal injury caused by negligence, and fraud.

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost profit or lost data, however arising.

Testing carries inherent risk. We work to minimise it — agreed windows, excluded techniques, an emergency stop — but you should take a verified backup before any engagement begins.

Data processing

Where an engagement involves personal data, we act as processor and you as controller. A Data Processing Agreement is executed alongside the statement of work. In summary, we:

  • Process personal data only on your documented instructions
  • Bind everyone with access to confidentiality
  • Apply encryption at rest and in transit to all evidence
  • Engage no sub-processor without your prior written authorisation
  • Assist with data-subject requests and with your Article 32 obligations
  • Notify you of any personal-data breach without undue delay, and within 24 hours of becoming aware
  • Delete or return all personal data at the end of the engagement
  • Submit to audit on reasonable notice

Request the full DPA from privacy@vapt-security.com. Standard Contractual Clauses are available where transfers require them. See also the privacy policy.
